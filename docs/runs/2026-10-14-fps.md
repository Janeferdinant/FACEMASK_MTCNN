## How to read this

- **Latency is uneven.** MTCNN takes most of a frame budget when it runs, so frames on which it runs are slow and the other frames are fast. The mean FPS is high, but the picture pauses briefly every N frames; the p95 columns show that.
- **Agreement measures the shortcut, not the model.** It compares the streaming pipeline with the same MTCNN and classifier run on every frame. It says nothing about whether either is right. The classifier's accuracy is in the README (Results).
- **Not a webcam.** The clip is dataset photos with a smooth zoom and pan: faces are small (30 to 130 px), there is no motion blur and nobody moves or puts on a mask. A real camera, other lighting and a lower-power CPU will give other numbers. This machine is a desktop CPU; a laptop will be slower.
- **New faces and new mask states wait for the next detection**, up to N-1 frames (N-1 at 30 fps is about 300 ms for N=10). Boxes between detections follow optical flow and keep their size.
- **Classes.** Label ids: 0 = without_mask, 1 = with_mask, 2 = mask_weared_incorrect.
